    07 Questions
    
      
    Got a question?
    Just ask.
    
      Browse every answer in the help center
    
    
    
    
      
    Do I need a designer or developer?
    No. You tell us about your business and myCROI builds your site, written for your trade. You can change anything you like afterwards in myCenter.
    
      
    What trades do you work with?
    Home and local service businesses: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, landscaping, roofing, pest control, pool service, handyman and detailing, from one-person crews to larger teams.
    
      
    What does each plan cost?
    Launch is $49 a month, Grow is $129 and Scale is $249. Pay yearly and you get two months free.
    
      
    Do I get a free web address?
    Yes. Every site gets a free yourname.mycroi.com address. You can use it on its own or connect your own domain later.
    
      
    Can I edit my site myself?
    Yes. In myCenter you can edit your site's pages, sections, photos and colors. You can also ask AI to rewrite one section: you see the old and new words side by side, and nothing changes until you pick the new ones.
    
      
    Will my site make things up about my business?
    No. Your site only says what you told us. It never invents reviews, guarantees, licenses or prices.
    
      
    How do my customers pay my invoices?
    On Scale, every invoice has a Pay now button. Customers pay by card on a secure page on your website and the money goes to your own Stripe account. You can also mark cash and check payments as paid.
    
      
    Can I cancel anytime?
    Yes. You can cancel anytime, from your account or by contacting us.
